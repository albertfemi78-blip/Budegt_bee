01 · BUDGETING BASICS

Know where your money goes.

Understand the building blocks of a personal budget before you start making decisions.

💰

Income

Money you receive, such as allowance, salary, gifts or a side-hustle payment.

🧾

Expenses

Money you spend on goods, services, bills, transport, food and other activities.

🏠

Needs

Essential costs required for basic living, education and safety.

🎮

Wants

Things that improve comfort or entertainment but can usually be delayed.

🐷

Savings

Money intentionally set aside for goals, emergencies or future plans.

03 · 50-30-20 RULE

Turn one income number into a simple plan.

The 50/30/20 rule is a budgeting guideline: 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings or debt goals.

06 · MONEY MISTAKES

Learn from common student scenarios.

Spending first and saving “whatever is left”

Scenario: A student spends their allowance throughout the month and rarely saves.

Try instead: Set aside a realistic savings amount near the beginning of the month.

Ignoring small recurring expenses

Scenario: Small daily purchases look harmless but add up.

Try instead: Track them for one week and review the total.

Buying because of pressure

Scenario: Friends encourage a purchase that was not in the plan.

Try instead: Use the 24-hour pause and check whether the purchase fits your wants budget.

Having no emergency buffer

Scenario: An unexpected school or transport cost appears.

Try instead: Build a small emergency reserve gradually.
